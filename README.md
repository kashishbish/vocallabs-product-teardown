## Executive Summary

I conducted a comprehensive product audit of Vocallabs.ai by:
- ✅ Exploring the website (homepage, solutions, about, contact)
- ✅ Attempting to schedule a demo
- ✅ Analyzing competitor positioning (Twilio comparison)
- ✅ Reviewing use cases and market positioning
- ✅ Researching India-first market opportunity

**Key Finding:** Vocallabs has strong product differentiation but GTM execution gaps in form friction, pricing transparency, customer support, and India market visibility.
